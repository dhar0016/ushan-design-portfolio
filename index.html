HELLO AGAIN

A thoughtful eye.
A curious mind.

I’m Nawela Ushan Dharmawardena, a designer with a background in computing and a diploma in Interactive Media Design from Algonquin College, where I earned Dean’s Honours recognition.

My experience includes UI/UX design at Cloudtrix Solutions, graphic design at Queensbury Hotel, and freelance client work through Fiverr. I enjoy the space where visual creativity meets a clear, practical purpose.

Outside design, I’m drawn to photography, nature, and music. They keep me observing, exploring, and seeing things from a different angle.
