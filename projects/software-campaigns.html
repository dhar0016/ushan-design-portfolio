Complex services. Clear messages.

Business software explained through bold headlines, feature lists, and product-led imagery.

Software promotion · Bilingual layout

THE PROJECT

Software & business campaigns

Promotional graphics for sales, distribution, courier management, and business systems. Sinhala and English copy share the same layout, alongside interfaces, illustrations, and contact details.

MY DESIGN APPROACH

I give the service name the strongest typographic emphasis, group features into compact lists, and use device imagery or illustrations to explain the product. Contact information forms the final level in the reading order.

  • Bilingual typography
  • Feature hierarchy
  • Service communication

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Explain the software category, highlight useful features and give interested customers a clear contact route.

Objective interpreted from the artwork; original customer brief not supplied.

COLOUR & CONTRAST

Representative colours

Navy and yellow create a strong light–dark contrast. Purple, red and gold accents distinguish different campaigns; repeated colour links headlines, illustrations and action areas.
