THE PROJECT

Character illustration study

A personal illustration study based on the established Fred Flintstone character. The piece brings together an enlarged drawing and a smaller reference-style presentation within one composition.

MY DESIGN APPROACH

Bold outlines and flat areas of colour preserve the recognisable cartoon style. The study concentrates on proportion, expression, and a clean separation between the character and background.

  • Digital drawing
  • Line and shape
  • Character study

Personal study of an existing character; the character is not an original creation.

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Study the proportions, outline and expression of an established cartoon character.

Objective interpreted from the artwork; original customer brief not supplied.

COLOUR & CONTRAST

Representative colours

Orange and blue offer complementary contrast, while a light background and strong black outlines separate the character from its surroundings.
