THE PROJECT

Service & recruitment promotions

A collection of promotional design samples. Each piece has a different subject, but shares a practical communication task: introduce a service or opportunity, make key details easy to scan, and identify the next action.

MY DESIGN APPROACH

I structure each composition around one prominent headline, followed by supporting details and a contact or action area. Photography establishes the subject while colour and scale separate the offer from the background.

  • Offer hierarchy
  • Image-led composition
  • Calls to action

Portfolio design samples. Offers, contact details and immigration-related statements in the historical artwork are not current advice or verified service claims.

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Communicate the offer or vacancy and make the next action easy to find.

Objective interpreted from the artwork; original customer brief not supplied.

COLOUR & CONTRAST

Representative colours

The travel design pairs white with gold; repair graphics use warm orange for emphasis; recruitment uses restrained greys. Colour supports the subject rather than forcing one palette onto every service.
