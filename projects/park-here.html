Less searching. More arriving.

A parking app interface built around a clear welcome, sign-up, and map experience.

Mobile interface · Figma

THE PROJECT

Park Here

Park Here is a mobile interface concept for finding and arranging parking. The available screens show the welcome page, account creation, and map view, with a consistent visual language throughout.

Open Figma prototype (opens in a new tab)

MY DESIGN APPROACH

Teal anchors the navigation and identity, while coral highlights the main actions. Simple form fields and a prominent map help separate account setup from the task of finding a parking location.

  • Interface design
  • Account creation flow
  • Map-based navigation

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Support account setup and a clear route to locating parking.

Objective interpreted from the artwork; original customer brief not supplied.

COLOUR & CONTRAST

Representative colours

Teal supplies a consistent base; coral creates warm–cool contrast for key actions. Pale backgrounds separate forms and map information.
