A story before the first scene

Poster design · Image composition

THE PROJECT

Ocean Love — poster study

A poster design study for Ocean Love, combining portraits and a coastal setting. The composition explores how a small number of visual elements can establish the tone of a story.

MY DESIGN APPROACH

Layered images connect the characters to the setting. A restrained palette gives the poster a soft atmosphere, while a large title and smaller supporting details establish a familiar poster hierarchy.

  • Poster layout
  • Image blending
  • Typography

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Establish the atmosphere of a fictional film through character, setting and title.

Objective interpreted from the artwork; original customer brief not supplied.

COLOUR & CONTRAST

Representative colours

Cool coastal tones and muted warm skin tones create a gentle warm–cool balance. The restrained palette keeps the layered images visually connected.
