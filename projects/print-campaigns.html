GRAPHIC DESIGN / 05

From first glance to fine detail.

Property flyers and an event invitation with a clear headline-to-detail reading order.

Flyers · Invitations · Print layout

THE PROJECT

Print flyers & event invitations

Printed promotional layouts presented as mockups, alongside a corporate event invitation. Large photographs or a central image introduce the subject; smaller text carries the practical information.

MY DESIGN APPROACH

I use the visual as an entry point and organise the copy into distinct sections. Consistent alignment and controlled spacing help the longer messages remain structured within a small print format.

  • Print hierarchy
  • Image and text balance
  • Event information

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Balance a strong first impression with enough supporting information for a reader to act.

Objective interpreted from the artwork; original customer brief not supplied.

COLOUR & CONTRAST

Representative colours

Deep navy and charcoal make light lettering and property imagery stand out. Muted warm accents separate featured information from dense supporting copy.
