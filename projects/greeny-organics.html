THE PROJECT

Greeny Organics

Greeny Organics is an interface concept for a business offering Nigerian food products. The design brief focuses on making fresh, frozen, and dried products easier to browse, and giving customers a clearer route to ordering and contacting the business.

Open Figma prototype (opens in a new tab)

MY DESIGN APPROACH

The project brings product discovery, ordering, and customer communication into one mobile experience. The intended experience is approachable and familiar, with attention to the needs of customers looking for Nigerian food.

  • Mobile interface concept
  • Product browsing
  • Customer communication

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Help customers browse food products, order and contact the business.

Objective interpreted from the artwork; original customer brief not supplied.

COLOUR & CONTRAST

Representative colours

The portfolio cover uses green with a pale neutral to reference the project name and food theme. These are presentation colours; view the Figma prototype for the actual interface palette.
