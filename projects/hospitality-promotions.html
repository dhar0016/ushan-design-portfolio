Event promotion · Digital design

THE PROJECT

Hospitality & event promotions

A collection of hospitality graphics, including sports-screening promotions and food offers. The designs use prominent subjects, venue messaging, and layered imagery to communicate an event or offer at a glance.

MY DESIGN APPROACH

High-contrast lettering and vivid colour help establish the key message. Supporting details sit below or around the main visual so the composition can balance an energetic mood with practical promotional information.

  • Event graphics
  • Image compositing
  • Promotional hierarchy

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Put the event or hospitality offer first, then make supporting details and venue information accessible.

Objective interpreted from the artwork; original customer brief not supplied.

COLOUR & CONTRAST

Representative colours

Purple and gold provide a rich dark–light contrast; warm accents pick out event headlines. The wedding graphic uses red and gold to echo its photographic subject.
